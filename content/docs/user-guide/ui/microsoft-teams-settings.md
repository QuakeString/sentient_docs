> **Note for 4.3.0.57:** messages to Microsoft Teams are not sent yet. Notification rules let you choose Microsoft Teams, but SENTIENT does not deliver to it, and the Microsoft Teams rule node cannot start. A later version will add it.

Until then, send alarms by [email, SMS, Telegram or WhatsApp](/docs/user-guide/notification-channels/). If your team must see alarms in Teams, have SENTIENT email them to the channel's email address (in Teams: the channel's **⋯** menu › **Get email address**).
