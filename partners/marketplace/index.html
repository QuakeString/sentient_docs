SENTIENT Marketplace

SENTIENT is about to launch a Software Marketplace to distribute custom SENTIENT components like widgets, pre-configured dashboards, rule chains, and other items. The Marketplace will soon be available in all CE and PE installations.

For Customers

Marketplace will allow you to explore IoT solutions and SENTIENT components provided by 100+ SENTIENT Contributors and Technology Partners.

For Sellers

We welcome you to become a seller at SENTIENT Marketplace and distribute your free and paid products to thousands of SENTIENT customers. This is an early call to submit information about your custom products. We will use this information to prepare the Marketplace.

The early birds will get priority listing and a minimum marketplace fee for paid products. Top publishers of free content will get valuable gifts from SENTIENT Team on a monthly basis: up to 100% license discounts, support, and other rewards.
